# TTS Open Platform Glossary

Use these terms for external-facing TikTok Shop Open Platform and TTSPC explanations.

| Term | Meaning | Aliases / Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3PL | Shipped by seller. | Third-party logistics context. |
| 4PL | Shipped by platform. | Platform-managed logistics context. |
| Developer shop | Sandbox or test shop used for development. | Sandbox, development shop, test shop. |
| CB | Cross-border shop. | Cross-border merchant/shop. |
| Local shop | Local seller shop. | Internal shorthand may call this "本本"; avoid that wording for external users. |
| bundle | Combined product set. | `combined_skus`; group or bundle product. |
